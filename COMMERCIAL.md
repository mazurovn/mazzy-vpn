- **personal use** (individuals, home labs),
- **research and science** (public and academic research organizations),
- **education** (schools, universities, students, teachers),
- **charitable / non-profit** organizations.

Any **noncommercial purpose** is permitted at no cost.

## When you need a commercial license

You need a separate **commercial license** if you use Mazzy VPN for a
**commercial purpose**, including (non-exhaustive):

- use inside a for-profit company's operations or products,
- bundling or redistributing Mazzy VPN in a paid product or paid service,
- offering Mazzy VPN (or a derivative) as a hosted/managed service (SaaS),
- any use that is primarily intended for commercial advantage.

The commercial license removes the noncommercial restriction and can include
priority support and indemnification terms.

## Enterprise edition (coming)

An **Enterprise edition** is planned on top of the open core, adding:

- centralized fleet management and policy control,
- SSO / SCIM and role-based access,
- audit logging and compliance reporting,
- managed AI-native control plane, grants and org-wide egress policies,
- SLA-backed support.

Enterprise is offered under a commercial license, separate from this
source-available core.
